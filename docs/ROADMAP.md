# Pact Roadmap

## Phase 1 — Foundation & User Setup [COMPLETED]
- Sign up, Sign in, Profile setup, Category selection (Study, Fitness), Commitment creation.

## Phase 2 — Human Matching [COMPLETED]
- Deterministic candidate evaluation, 2-proposal/7-day Free limit, single best proposal presentation, mutual acceptance.

## Phase 3 — Daily Accountability [COMPLETED]
- Today screen hero, cutoff evaluation (Waiting vs Overdue), 10-second Done/Partial/Missed check-in, partner status, lightweight sync.

## Phase 4 — Recovery, Rematching & Solo [COMPLETED]
- Missed commitment triggers 24h recovery protocol, 4 recovery actions, partner sees "Recovery in progress", Respectful Rematch and Continue Solo.

## Phase 5 — Reliability, Cycle, History & Pact Assist [COMPLETED]
- Trust index calculation, cycle day progression, cycle completion summary, basic pact history, 48h Pact Assist bridge.

## Phase 6 — QA, Verification & Production Hardening [IN PROGRESS]
- Complete 67-item UAT audit, security review, concurrency testing, bug fixing, regression tests.
