# Pact Engineering & Product Constitution

## Primary UX Principle
> **Pact UX Principle: Reduce decisions, not functionality.**
>
> Pact may have sophisticated matching, reliability, recovery, scheduling and accountability logic underneath, but users should experience only the minimum information required to act.
>
> Every screen must answer one primary question and have one primary action.
>
> Prefer progressive disclosure over showing every available metric.
>
> Never expose backend sophistication as UI complexity.
>
> The default user journey: **Open → See commitment → Check in → See partner → Leave.**

## Strict Rules for Free V1
1. **One User, One Commitment, One Active Pact**: No multi-pact clutter.
2. **No Public Match Marketplace**: Do not expose a long catalog of people to shop through. Show one proposal at a time.
3. **Free 2-Proposal / 7-Day Limit**: Track proposals used strictly.
4. **Pact Assist is a 48h Bridge, NOT an AI Companion**:
   - Condition A (48h without match) is strictly independent of Condition B (proposal limit exhausted).
   - Scoped strictly to: Plan, Start, Check, Recover.
   - Human matching continues in the background.
5. **No Anti-AI Slop**:
   - No decorative blobs, unnecessary gradients, or nested cards.
   - No vanity gamification (no streaks on Today, no leaderboards).
   - Touch targets must be accessible (>=44px).
   - Typography must be clean and calm.
